About Heathvale Orchards

Heathvale Orchards is a small orchard located in Heathcote, on the Port Hills of Christchurch, Canterbury. We grow a range of spray and chemical free fruit and vegetables, available seasonally for pickup directly from the orchard.

Our plums include several varieties – Shiro, Billington, Burbank, Black Doris, and Purple King – which ripen at different times through summer. We also grow feijoas in autumn, and may have other seasonal produce available throughout the year.

We serve customers from across Christchurch and Canterbury who are looking for locally grown, spray and chemical free produce. Pickup is from 112 Bridle Path Road, Heathcote – call or text ahead to check availability.
